GILLYLAB — Custom Simulator

For each category — striking, grappling, and so on — you answer two simple questions: who's better, and by how much? Then, separately, how much does it matter in this matchup? A tick mark shows what the GillyLab model already thinks, so you can tell right away whether you're agreeing with it or going against it. Everything updates live as you move the sliders, so you always see the new win% before you commit to it — and your result stays side-by-side with the model's original pick, so you can compare the two at a glance.
