FIGHT_HISTORY data integrity — open issues
==========================================
Found 2026-07-15 while building the Six Degrees prototype. Filed here because
they outlive that prototype: FIGHT_HISTORY backs the scouting report, common
opponents, lite profiles, and the matchup pages.

Surfaced by a playtester, not by a test:
  "it was showing charles johnson and brendan allen had a common opponent of
   bruno silva ... it is confusing middleweight bruno silva with flyweight
   bruno gustavo da silva"

Root cause in one line: NAMES ARE NOT IDENTITIES. Every consumer of
FIGHT_HISTORY matches fighters by normalised name, and that silently welds two
different people together whenever they share one.


ISSUE 1 — the middleweight Bruno "Blindado" Silva has no record  [REAL, OPEN]
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
There are two real fighters:
  Bruno "Bulldog" Gustavo da Silva   flyweight
  Bruno "Blindado" Silva             middleweight

FIGHT_HISTORY has keys "Bruno Gustavo da Silva" and "Bruno Silva" — but BOTH
hold the FLYWEIGHT's record (see issue 2). The middleweight has no key at all.
He exists only as the opponent string "Bruno Silva" inside other fighters'
records (Brendan Allen, Jun 24 2023, UFC on ABC: Emmett vs. Topuria).

So any code resolving "Bruno Silva" -> a fighter lands on the flyweight, and
the middleweight's bouts get attributed to a man 40lb lighter.

Impact:
  - Six Degrees: invented an edge Brendan Allen — (flyweight) Bruno Silva,
    which is what the playtester caught. FIXED there by a reciprocity gate.
  - The app's common-opponents: currently SAFE, but only by luck. It intersects
    opponent NAME STRINGS, and Johnson's record happens to say "Bruno Gustavo
    da Silva" while Allen's says "Bruno Silva". If both records had used the
    short form, the app would print a fake common opponent. This is an accident,
    not a defence.

Fix: add the middleweight as his own key, and make the ingest disambiguate.
Needs a source decision — probably an explicit alias/identity map rather than
more string heuristics.


ISSUE 2 — duplicate keys for one fighter  [REAL, OPEN]
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Bruno Silva" and "Bruno Gustavo da Silva" are the SAME flyweight, stored twice:
  27 bouts each, 12 UFC each, 89% identical by date+result.
The 3 "differences" are date noise on the same three opponents:
  Ulanbekov   Oct 10, 2020  vs  Oct 11, 2020
  Khalid Taha Oct  5, 2019  vs  Oct  6, 2019
  Ralph Acosta Oct 27, 2018 vs  Dec 15, 2018   (WFF 40 / World Fighting Federation 40)

Two more duplicate pairs, accent-only, caught by normalised-name comparison:
  ["Viviane Araujo", "Viviane Araújo"]
  ["Jose Aldo", "José Aldo"]

These are the same class as the RDA duplicates merged earlier in the project.
The Aldo/Araujo pairs are trivially safe to merge. The Bruno pair is NOT — it's
entangled with issue 1, since collapsing them still leaves the middleweight
homeless and his fights mis-attributed. Fix issue 1 first.


ISSUE 3 — 691 one-sided links  [MOSTLY BENIGN, WORTH AUDITING]
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A genuine bout is reciprocal: it appears in both corners' records. Across
FIGHT_HISTORY:
    reciprocal   25,540
    one-sided       691   (2.6%)

One-sided links are a mix of:
  (a) name collisions   — two people, one name (issue 1). These are BUGS.
  (b) aliases           — one person, two spellings, so each record names the
                          other differently. e.g. "Anderson Berinja" claims 8
                          bouts that none of his opponents' records echo.
                          These are real bouts under a wrong/alt name.

Six Degrees now drops all 691 (the gate is cheap and a fake edge breaks a
puzzle, while a missing edge just adds a hop). The app should NOT blanket-drop
them — it would lose real history. Worth an audit to split (a) from (b) and
build a proper alias map.

Reproduce:
    node -e '<reciprocity scan>'   # see scripts/gen-sixdegrees-data.cjs,
                                   # the `agreed()` helper


WHAT THE PROTOTYPE DOES ABOUT IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
scripts/gen-sixdegrees-data.cjs gates every edge on reciprocity: both corners
must name each other, whenever both keep records. If the opponent has no record
we can't check and we keep the edge (the ordinary case for regional opponents).
Drops 673 links from the board. Regression-tested in scripts/test-sixdegrees.cjs
under "== name collisions ==", which asserts the specific Allen/Johnson case AND
the general invariant that no one-sided edge survives.
