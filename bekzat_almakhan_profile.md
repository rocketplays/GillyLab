## Corrections to Common Errors in Published Bios

1. **UFC debut date listed as "February 17, 2024"** (ConanDaily): Incorrect. UFC Fight Night 238 was **March 2, 2024**.
2. **Record listed as 12-2**: Outdated — he lost to Aleksandre Topuria at UFC FN 265 (Nov 22, 2025), making the correct record **12-3-0**.
3. **"Pro since 2016"** (per UFC.com): Refers to exhibition/Zhekpe Zhek bouts, not official MMA pro record. His first pro MMA fight was July 28, 2018.
4. **Birthplace discrepancy**: Sherdog and some sources say Akzhar/Bayzak District/Jambyl Region; ConanDaily says Uzynagash/Zhambyl District/Almaty Region. Both may partially refer to the same rural area near Taraz. UFC page only specifies Kazakhstan. Use with caution.
5. **Gym attribution**: UFC.com and official bios don't specify a gym, but ConanDaily and multiple sources confirm **777 Fight Club and Gym** in Almaty. Sherdog lists association as **Triumph Fights** (possibly a management/team name rather than gym).
