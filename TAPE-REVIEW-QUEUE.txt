Videos that are probably the real fight but that I would not place without a
person confirming. Nothing here is in index.html. Delete a line once you have
either placed it or ruled it out.

HOW I DECIDED WHAT TO PLACE vs FLAG
Place: the channel is the rights-holder (promotion or UFC Fight Pass) AND the
       title names both fighters, so the bout is unambiguous.
Flag:  right channel, but the title does not identify WHICH fight it is — the
       channel earns trust, the title does not identify the bout.
Reject and do not record: reupload channels, and anything that smells of a video
       game ("UFC 4", "AI Simulation", "CPU vs CPU", "Gameplay").

── HOW TO SEARCH, AND ONE THING THAT DOES NOT WORK ──────────────────────────

YOUTUBE'S OWN CHANNEL SEARCH IS NOT EXHAUSTIVE. Do not rely on it. Searching
    youtube.com/@BellatorMMA/search?query=Njokuani
returns TWO videos and stops — scrolling adds nothing — yet BellatorMMA also
hosts his Kato and Koreshkov fights, which a general web search found on that
same channel. The channel-scoped harvest that worked so well on Fight Pass and
Paramount+ does NOT transfer here.

What works instead, per bout:
    WebSearch restricted to youtube.com, query "<Fighter> vs <Opponent> <event>"
    then oembed the result and check author_name.
It is one search plus one verification per fight. Slow, but it is the only
method that actually finds things.

YIELD from YouTube alone, measured on UFC 330: about 14 of 180 pre-UFC bouts.
  HAVE CHANNELS, worth searching: Bellator, LFA (also carries RFA and Legacy FC),
      Invicta FC, Cage Fury, Cage Titans, Shooto Brazil.
  NO USABLE FOOTAGE: WLMMA (Germany, 8 Stoltzfus bouts), Xtreme Knockout (6 Neal
      bouts), PA Cage Fight, CES MMA, and most one-off regional cards.


── THE BIGGEST MISS I MADE, AND THE FIX ─────────────────────────────────────

I spent a long time on YouTube before checking the obvious thing: FIGHT PASS
ITSELF CARRIES THE REGIONAL PROMOTIONS. Invicta, Cage Fury, LFA, RFA, Legacy FC
and Shooto Brazil are all Fight Pass libraries. I had been running Fight Pass
searches all session but only ever reading the UFC results out of them.

    Plain fighter search on ufcfightpass.com/search returns their PRE-UFC bouts
    too. "Jeremiah Wells" returns his CFFC 44 / 56 / 68 / 78 fights directly.

That one realisation took UFC 330 from 166 unlinked to 138, more than the entire
YouTube effort. DO THIS FIRST on any future card; treat YouTube as the
free-link upgrade pass afterwards, not the primary source.

Also worth knowing: the curated "<Promotion> Alumni: <Fighter>" playlists are a
SUBSET of what the search returns. Use both.
