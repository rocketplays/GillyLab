TAPE STUDY — CANDIDATES FLAGGED FOR HUMAN REVIEW
Videos that are probably the real fight but that I would not place without a
person confirming. Nothing here is in index.html. Delete a line once you have
either placed it or ruled it out.

HOW I DECIDED WHAT TO PLACE vs FLAG
Place: the channel is the rights-holder (promotion or UFC Fight Pass) AND the
       title names both fighters, so the bout is unambiguous.
Flag:  right channel, but the title does not identify WHICH fight it is — the
       channel earns trust, the title does not identify the bout.
Reject and do not record: reupload channels, and anything that smells of a video
       game ("UFC 4", "AI Simulation", "CPU vs CPU", "Gameplay").

── HOW TO SEARCH, AND ONE THING THAT DOES NOT WORK ──────────────────────────

YOUTUBE'S OWN CHANNEL SEARCH IS NOT EXHAUSTIVE. Do not rely on it. Searching
    youtube.com/@BellatorMMA/search?query=Njokuani
returns TWO videos and stops — scrolling adds nothing — yet BellatorMMA also
hosts his Kato and Koreshkov fights, which a general web search found on that
same channel. The channel-scoped harvest that worked so well on Fight Pass and
Paramount+ does NOT transfer here.

What works instead, per bout:
    WebSearch restricted to youtube.com, query "<Fighter> vs <Opponent> <event>"
    then oembed the result and check author_name.
It is one search plus one verification per fight. Slow, but it is the only
method that actually finds things.

YIELD from YouTube alone, measured on UFC 330: about 14 of 180 pre-UFC bouts.
  HAVE CHANNELS, worth searching: Bellator, LFA (also carries RFA and Legacy FC),
      Invicta FC, Cage Fury, Cage Titans, Shooto Brazil.
  I WROTE THIS LIST WRONG THE FIRST TIME. I had "WLMMA (Germany) and Xtreme
  Knockout — no usable footage" here, on the strength of one search each. Both
  run their OWN YouTube channels:
      Xtreme Knockout  @XtremeKnockout   — "XKO 34 Kevin Holland vs Geoff Neal"
      We Love MMA      channel/UCSGtArCD1lW2FcxP-P3kYrA  (German, @welovemma
                       does NOT resolve — use the channel-id URL)
  Do not conclude a regional promotion has no footage from a fighter-name search
  alone. Search the EVENT NAME too; small promotions title by event and often
  post years of back catalogue.


── THE BIGGEST MISS I MADE, AND THE FIX ─────────────────────────────────────

I spent a long time on YouTube before checking the obvious thing: FIGHT PASS
ITSELF CARRIES THE REGIONAL PROMOTIONS. Invicta, Cage Fury, LFA, RFA, Legacy FC
and Shooto Brazil are all Fight Pass libraries. I had been running Fight Pass
searches all session but only ever reading the UFC results out of them.

    Plain fighter search on ufcfightpass.com/search returns their PRE-UFC bouts
    too. "Jeremiah Wells" returns his CFFC 44 / 56 / 68 / 78 fights directly.

That one realisation took UFC 330 from 166 unlinked to 138, more than the entire
YouTube effort. DO THIS FIRST on any future card; treat YouTube as the
free-link upgrade pass afterwards, not the primary source.

Also worth knowing: the curated "<Promotion> Alumni: <Fighter>" playlists are a
SUBSET of what the search returns. Use both.

  ── PASS 4: THE OR'd-QUERY MISTAKE ──────────────────────────────────────────
  Pass 3's "dead end" list was partly wrong, and the cause is worth recording.
  I searched `"Luque" Lisboa OR "Yuri Moura" OR "Alfredo Souza"` — three bouts
  crammed into one query — and for Magny I searched no opponent name at all,
  just "regional MMA 2011". Both returned the fighter's UFC career and I read
  that as absence. This file already said the method is ONE SEARCH PER BOUT.
  I did not follow it. Re-running it properly, one opponent per query, found
  three things in ten minutes:

      Neil Magny vs Nolan Norwood      — I had called Magny a total dead end
      Gillian Robertson vs Calvillo    — Global Knock-Out's own channel
      Abdul-Malik vs Saint-Gelais      — free copy of a bout I had paywalled

  An OR'd query is not N searches. YouTube ranks on the whole string and the
  famous name dominates it. If a query names more than one bout, it has tested
  no bout.

  TWO MORE SOURCE TYPES THIS SURFACED, neither of which is a promotion:
      THE FIGHTER'S OWN CHANNEL. @88amagny has Magny vs Norwood and nothing
      else, split RD1 / RD2 / winner as three uploads. Placed RD1 — partial,
      but real. Checked @luquevicente and @EstebanRibovicsUFC the same way:
      both are vlog channels, no old fights. Cheap to check, occasionally pays.
      THE UFC FIGHT PASS YOUTUBE CHANNEL carries LFA cards in full and free —
      "LFA 176: Mansur Abdul-Malik vs Allan Saint-Gelais | February 9, 2024".
      Always check it before settling for a ufcfightpass.com/video link.

  ONE TRAP WORTH KNOWING: oembed returns literal "Forbidden" for videos with
  embedding disabled or removed. That is NOT a rate limit and NOT a verdict on
  the channel — four of the Gaziev/Stošić results did it while other ids
  resolved fine in the same second. Treat it as "cannot verify", not "bad".
  Gaziev vs Stošić (Brave CF 69): the only resolvable copies are MMA Savez
  Srbije and Real Fight Balkans — neither is the rights holder. Left unplaced.

  ALSO: search-result titles lie. The Dailidko video's search title said
  "BRAVE CF 81"; oembed said "BRAVE CF 65", which is what our record has.
  Always take the title from oembed, never from the search snippet.

── PASS 6: SEARCH THE NATIVE-SCRIPT NAME ───────────────────────────────────
THE SINGLE HIGHEST-LEVERAGE TRICK FOUND SO FAR. Asian promotions title their
uploads in their own script. A romanised query does not match those titles at
all, so every earlier pass reported "no footage" for fights that were sitting
in public on the promotion's own channel.

Look the fighter's native name up first, then search THAT:

    Aoriqileng     傲日其楞      Sumudaerji   苏木达尔基
    Song Yadong    宋亚东        Yan Xiaonan  闫晓楠
    Kai Asakura    朝倉海        Kyoji Horiguchi 堀口恭司
    Rei Tsuruya    鶴屋怜        Seokhyeon Ko 고석현

Also translate the PROMOTION. This is what unlocked WLF: its MMA arm is
武林笼中对 ("Wulin cage bouts"), NOT 武林风, which is the kickboxing show. I had
searched only the kickboxing name and concluded WLF had no MMA catalogue.

  CHANNELS CONFIRMED, all rights holders:
      ROAD FIGHTING CHAMPIONSHIP  @roadfc — bilingual hangul+romaji titles,
          "XIAOMI ROAD FC 034 Yan Xiaonan(얜샤오난) VS Fujino Emi(후지노 에미)".
          Deep archive, back to at least ROAD FC 023. Best of the three.
      DEEP チャンネル  @deep2001MMA — "DEEP 100 IMPACT 鶴屋怜VS竜己"
      AFC엔젤스파이팅 챔피언십  @afc78 — "[AFC16] 웰터급 챔피언 결정전 고석현 VS 안재영"

  ONE NAMING TRAP: fighters are billed by RING NAME in native-language titles.
  Tsuruya's DEEP 100 opponent is 竜己 ("Tatsuki"); our record says Tatsuki
  Okano. Same bout — matched on event + date, not on the name. Check the date
  before rejecting a title whose opponent name looks wrong.
  A SECOND ONE: ROAD FC runs a numbered main card and a YOUNG GUNS card on the
  same night. "ROAD FC YOUNG GUNS 32" IS "ROAD FC 037", 11 Mar 2017. Verify the
  date rather than assuming the number mismatch means a different fight.

  STILL DEAD even in native script: WLF's 2016-2019 W.A.R.S./E.P.I.C. archive.
  The 武林笼中对 playlist that exists is all recent YFU-era cards. Aoriqileng
  (30), Song Yadong (17), Sumudaerji (16) remain the biggest untouched block.

  A WRONG LINK THIS PASS CAUGHT, worth noting because it was already live:
  Seokhyeon Ko's AFC 17 row (vs Yun Jae Jung) held the AFC 16 video. Both are
