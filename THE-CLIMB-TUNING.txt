The Climb — tuning state, and the one piece left
================================================================================
2026-07-15, second pass. Everything below is MEASURED, not guessed. Read the
arithmetic first; it's the thing five separate tuning attempts kept rediscovering
the hard way.

The previous pass's headline lesson was right and it paid off again immediately:
MAKE IT DO THE ARITHMETIC BEFORE IT TOUCHES A DIAL. Doing the division first
turned "fix (c): wrestling's edge needs damping" — a dial — into "the ground buys
the same rating for 17 fewer points" — a structural bug that no dial would have
fixed. Same afternoon, one division, four fifths of a 10x gap.

Tools now include scripts/climb-arithmetic.cjs (ladder shape, what a budget buys,
the loss budget) and scripts/check-climb-cap.cjs (does MAX_FAVORITE actually bite,
measured in play). Run the first one BEFORE forming a theory.


THE ARITHMETIC — write this on the wall
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    15 wins ÷ 5 losses  ⟹  you must be a ~76% favorite in every fight
    76%                 ⟹  the exact fight that feels like theft to lose

Playtest: "losing 5 times at an average moderate favorite isn't wrong, but losing
5 times at 80% FEELS wrong." Correct, and it is a CONTRADICTION, not a tuning
miss. You cannot have "no heavy favorites" AND "a 15-win climb on a 5-loss
budget". Verified against a Monte Carlo this pass, 15 wins, CUT_AT 5:

    avg 76% -> 4.7 expected losses -> survive 51%   <- feels wrong
    avg 69% -> 6.7 expected losses -> survive 25%
    avg 65% -> 8.1 expected losses -> survive 15%
    8 wins @ 68% -> 3.8 losses -> survive 67%       <- the short ladder

CORRECTION to the last pass's table: it read 69% -> ~15% and 65% -> ~7%. Those
are wrong — they're the right numbers against the wrong row. The closed form and
a 400k-trial Monte Carlo agree to 0.1% (see the survive() note in
climb-arithmetic.cjs). The 76% -> 51% row, which is the load-bearing one, was
right, so the CONCLUSION stands: THE LADDER MUST BE SHORT. But a table nobody
re-derived carried two wrong cells into a second session, and they were the two
cells being used to argue "unwinnable".


BANKED AND WORKING — do not re-litigate
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * RATING TRACKS THE LADDER. RATING_MIN 30 / SPAN 150 / cap at champion+5.
    (SPAN was 168; re-fitted this pass because equal WEIGHTS changed what a point
    is worth. Same job — "~70 pts reaches ~100" — different number.)
  * ODDS ARE CALIBRATED. 6,000 fights binned: worst gap 6.5pts. The card tells
    the truth. Verify with the calibration snippet before ever blaming the dice —
    and read G.log[G.log.length-1], NOT G.log[0] (see MEASURING, below).
  * STYLE DECIDES THE BOARD. Style is centred on YOUR OWN AVERAGE, not 0.5 —
    centring on 0.5 double-counts quality and taxes specialists twice.
  * EVERY SLIDER IS WORTH 1/9. See "the styles were never equal", below. This
    REPLACES the old "feet 0.39 / ground 0.39" entry, which was measured on the
    wrong axis and was the real cause of fix (c).
  * Odds shown as a BAND before the fight, the number after it, with a variance
    note only when the result was genuinely improbable.
  * Submissions exist. Method follows the build (grappler: 42% SUB / 5% KO).
  * A CONTENDER who beat you never returns — the CHAMPION does, after you win one
    fight. Stating it precisely because the imprecise version ("the conqueror never
    returns") was true of the code, banked in a gate, and quietly meant the belt
    was unwinnable after a single title loss. See "THE RUN THAT COULDN'T END".
  * A loss costs a rung. (The design always said so; the code didn't until now.)
  * Every rung offers 2+ fights; 11 divisions play clean; ranks come from
    data/rankings.json; payload 443KB -> 19KB.
  * A ranked man is never offered a gatekeeper. (New this pass; see below.)


DONE THIS PASS — the short ladder, and what it cost
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. UNRANKED_WINS 5 -> 1. One gatekeeper fight, deliberately a gift.
 2. POINTS_START 20 -> 42. The trap, and the reason 1 works: the ladder jumps
    gatekeeper 46.9 -> #15 62.2, a 15.3-point CLIFF the tune-up phase was
    secretly bridging with points. Debut now rates 74 = a ~74% favorite over #15.
    Parts 1 and 2 are ONE change; either alone is worse than neither.
 3. STEP 1 -> 3. Multi-rung jumps: beating a man takes HIS rank.
 4. Sub-rank advancement (beat anyone at/below you, advance one rung) +
    MAX_FAVORITE 0.78.
 c. THE STYLES WERE NEVER EQUAL — and this, not the style triangle, was fix (c).
    0.39/0.39 balances what each style is worth WHEN MAXED. It does not balance
    what each style COSTS, and players spend points:
        feet   0.39 over FOUR sliders  -> 4 x 17 = 68 pts to own it
        ground 0.39 over THREE sliders -> 3 x 17 = 51 pts to own it
    Same ceiling, 17 fewer points. Measured best rating per budget, the ground led
    by +4.7 at 20 pts, +12.7 at 42, +15.5 at 50 — and 50 pts is where a
    championship run lives. At SCALE 26 that made a same-budget grappler an 80%
    favorite over a same-budget striker before the triangle said a word.
    Now every slider is 1/9: a point buys the same rating wherever it goes, style
    totals differ BY CONSTRUCTION (feet 4/9, ground 3/9) because the feet cost
    four sliders, and every archetype debuts on the same number.
    The bitter part: the weights this replaced (striking 0.44 / ground 0.35) were
    already near the 4:3 ratio that prices the styles equally. The playtest
    complaint that triggered the 0.39/0.39 change — "becoming a striking
    specialist weighs a lot more heavily" — was TRUE and was NOT a bug: maxing the
    feet weighed 26% more because it cost 33% more. The fix inverted a non-bug
    into a real one, and wrote "I set these by eye and checked the wrong axis" in
    the comment while doing it.
 a. FIXED. jump was null for unranked men, so a RANKED player beating one never
    moved. Now unranked carries jump=99 and sub-rank advancement handles it.
 b. FIXED, BUT NOT THE WAY THE NOTE SAID, AND NOT WHERE THE NOTE LOOKED. The
    proposed fix — "draw from the whole ladder" — does not work at all: a man you
    beat ten fights ago is a BIGGER gift now, because you have gained points since.
    The exhausted avail() pool was never the reason the cap lapsed. The reason was
    that a lost title fight deleted the win condition and the run never ended; see
    "THE RUN THAT COULDN'T END", below. Cap now measures 2.6% / 0.5% over.

    striker        3% -> 28%        striker/safe   0% -> 48%
    wrestler      30% -> 38%        striker/bold   5% -> 28%
    grappler      23% -> 38%        striker/blind     -> 38%
No strategy at 0%. Nothing running away. The 10x style gap is 1.4x. safe > blind >

  * A ranked man could be offered a GATEKEEPER. `src = near.length ? near : all`

THE RUN THAT COULDN'T END — one bug, four symptoms, none of them where I looked
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIXED. Writing down the hunt, because the hunt is the lesson.

Three things were failing and I treated them as three problems:
    MAX_FAVORITE lapsed        55% of ranked fights over the 0.78 cap
    rematches flooded          23-46% of boards vs a 20% gate
    the #1 treadmill lived     39-2 records, no belt, 40-fight guard
I patched two of them. Both patches were reasonable, both were measured, and both
were treating symptoms of a bug in a line I never suspected:

    const avail = f => !G.beat.has(f.name) && !fought.has(f.name);

"Exclude everyone you've FOUGHT" is right for contenders and catastrophic for the
belt. LOSE ONE TITLE FIGHT AND THE CHAMPION LEAVES THE POOL FOREVER. `title` is
then empty, the #1 board is three men you already beat (measured: `4@72 1@71
3@93`, no champion anywhere on it), and the run has NO WIN CONDITION. It cannot
end, so it runs to the guard; you keep earning, drift to rating 110 against an
85-power division, and then every card is a gift, the cap has nobody legal to swap
in, and a 20-fight run eats a 16-man ladder and reports rematches.

All four symptoms, one line. THE TELL WAS THE RUN LENGTH ALL ALONG — a game whose
runs won't end is a game whose win condition is unreachable, and I spent an hour
tuning the things downstream of that instead of asking why nobody was finishing.
WHEN A RUN WON'T END, CHECK THE WIN CONDITION BEFORE YOU TUNE ANYTHING ELSE.

And the gate BANKED the bug: `revenge===0` counted any conqueror on the board,
champion included, so the assertion was green BECAUSE the belt had been deleted
from the game. A test can bank a bug exactly as hard as it banks a fix.

Fixed together:
  * The champion is exempt from `fought`. A title loss costs a SHOT, not the run:
    you must win a fight to earn another crack at him, which keeps the Walker
    instant-rematch loop dead (that fix was about being handed your conqueror
    IMMEDIATELY — it was never a rule that the belt should be unreachable).
  * A LOSS COSTS A RUNG. CUT_AT's own comment has always said losses "cost you
    rank, time and a soft matchmaking step-down" — only the last two were ever
    implemented. Without it, a #1 who lost a title fight stayed #1, beat one gimme
    and got another shot: unlimited title fights at one fight apiece, measured at a
    90% belt rate. A free belt.
  * PAY FOR PROGRESS, NOT POSTURE (rewardFor). `altitude` paid for WHERE YOU ARE,
    so a gimme at #3 paid 2.1x and the identical gimme at #12 paid 1.35x, for the
    same one rung of progress. Its own comment called it: "climbing compounds: the
    higher you get the faster you grow" — the runaway loop, written down as a
    feature. A win now pays RUNG_PAY per rung it actually moves you, so grind (1
    rung x 15) and bold (3 rungs x 5) arrive at the same level and the gap you
    start with is the gap you keep.
  * The radius top-up widened SYMMETRICALLY over radii reaching 99, so an
    exhausted top of the ladder got topped up from #13 — rating 108.9 vs power
    67.1 at 95%. It now widens upward only, never past 3 rungs beneath you (the
    same weakness cap the 'below' band always had), and prefers a near-rank
    rematch over a stranger ten rungs down.
  * MAX_FAVORITE 0.78 -> 0.85, because 0.78 IS A CONTRADICTION — the same shape as
    the one at the top of this file, one level up. The board is below/near/up BY
    DESIGN; a 0.78 cap allows a 14.3-point gap; the debut is +11.9 over its own
    rung (the hyped-prospect premise) and the below card is ~3 rungs weaker again
    = +17.3 = 82%. The below card is over a 0.78 cap ON TURN ONE, by construction.
    Parts 2 and 4 of the last pass cannot both hold. The cap's job is to kill
    THEFT, not to flatten the risk gradient — flattening it deletes the safe option
    and the whole point of the choice.

    striker        3% -> 18%        striker/safe   0% -> 27%
    wrestler      30% -> 33%        striker/bold   5% -> 13%
    grappler      23% -> 37%        striker/blind     -> 21%

Spread 13-37%, nothing at 0%, nothing running away. safe (27%) > blind (21%) —

matchups, that should be an advantage, not the same as picking randomly."
Runs 11-15 fights (was 13-34). Records ~8-4 to 10-4 — a champion's record, which

All gates green. All 11 divisions play in 7-19 fights.

THE FIRST VERSION OF THIS TABLE WAS MEASURED AT N=30 AND TWO OF ITS CELLS WERE
NOISE. It read grappler 57% and safe 13%, and I wrote two "still open" problems

    "grappler 57% vs safe 13% is a 4x spread" ...... it is 37% vs 27%. 1.4x.

AND ONE OPEN QUESTION, recorded not fixed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMITTING IS NOT REWARDED, despite the upCost comment claiming it is. Measured
cumulative value per point, one slider from 1 to level L:
    L=2  0.0761   L=4  0.0769   L=6  0.0631   L=8  0.0636   L=10  0.0588
Efficiency DECLINES. 9->10 is the worst buy on the sheet (0.0517/pt); 3->4 is the
best (0.0852/pt). Spreading strictly beats committing, so "value slightly outruns
cost at the top, so committing is rewarded — a 10 is worth reaching" is false. It
was asserted, never measured, and sat in the file reading as evidence.
Also: maxing one attribute costs 17, not 18 (ATTR_MIN is 1 — nine steps, not ten),
so "three is impossible" is no longer true at a 42-pt start.
Left alone deliberately: it's a real design change, it wants its own measurement,
and the priority-order bots all commit anyway so it didn't block the ladder.


MEASURING — the tools, and how they lied to me
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    scripts/climb-arithmetic.cjs       ladder shape, budgets, loss budget. FIRST.
    scripts/check-climb-cap.cjs        does MAX_FAVORITE bite, measured in play
    scripts/sim-climb-runs.cjs         full careers per strategy (--only X)
    scripts/smoke-climb-divisions.cjs  all 11 divisions, is any belt free
    scripts/test-climb.cjs / -fixes    behaviour gates

  * A STATIC BOARD SEES NOTHING. Every bug that reached a player — the 50.0%
    column, the Walker rematch loop, the one-option board at #15 — was invisible
    to anything that didn't PLAY. Sample every rung of every division; bugs live
    at the edges (#15 and #1), and tests that check the middle miss them.
  * A TEST THAT CANNOT RUN CANNOT FAIL. Two gates were pinned to a dead sandbox
    path and threw EACCES for a whole session. Nobody noticed, because a suite
    that errors and a suite that passes both end with you moving on. RUN THE
    GATES ON THE BASELINE FIRST — if they don't pass there, they aren't gates.
  * G.log IS APPEND-ONLY. Reading G.log[0] in a multi-fight run reports the FIRST
    fight forever. It produced a beautiful, plausible, entirely wrong calibration
    table showing a 51-point failure that did not exist.
  * THIN SAMPLES PRODUCE CONFIDENT NONSENSE. "LHW is a free belt" came from THREE
    runs. At N=4 the same divisions read 25/50/25% across three settings — pure
    noise I nearly tuned against. N=25 still carries +-9%. (A single probe run
    this pass read 3-5 for a strategy whose true average is 16.6-3.4.)
    AND IT GOT ME AGAIN, IN THE SAME SESSION, AFTER I QUOTED IT. I ran the final
    sweep at N=30, read grappler 57% / safe 13%, and wrote two open problems out of
    two cells that were noise — a 4x style spread that is really 1.4x, and a
    "violated" playtest spec that is really satisfied. Knowing the rule is not the
    same as having a floor. THE FLOOR IS N=120 (SE ~3.5%) FOR ANY NUMBER YOU WRITE
    DOWN, and it costs ten seconds. Sweep wide at N=30 to find the shape; re-run at
    N=120 before you believe it, and always before you commit it.
  * DON'T SAMPLE THE FUNCTION, SAMPLE THE GAME. "3,273 of 6,000 fights at the 95%
    clamp" was random budgets against random ranks — mismatches no run produces.
    Real runs: 0.5%. I raised that alarm and had to withdraw it. The `easy` bot
    ducking the title for 28 boards is the same error wearing a bot's clothes.
  * A CLAIM OUTLIVES THE THING THAT MADE IT TRUE. Seven times now: two tip-box
    lines, the page subtitle, a test asserting the scorer we deleted, "X% per
    round" after the units changed, "Lives" hardcoded to 2 after CUT_AT hit 5, the
    RATING_SPAN knife-edge block (measured in a regime that no longer existed,
    sitting above a constant it did not describe and arguing for a third value),
    and the upCost "committing is rewarded" claim. I added one MYSELF this pass —
    wrote "42 lands the debut at 74 (striker)" from a measurement taken before I
    changed the weights, and it was wrong by the time the edit landed.
    When you remove a thing, grep for the assertions ABOUT it. When you change a
    number, re-read every comment that quotes one.
  * WHEN A FUNCTION CHANGES WHAT ITS RETURN VALUE MEANS, nothing throws and every
    caller quietly lies. winProb() went per-round -> per-fight and the bo3 bug
    shipped -13,700 odds. jump went `null` -> `99` for unranked men this pass; the
    branch that reads it had to change in the same commit or every gatekeeper win
    would have silently jumped the player to rank 99.
  * A RED GATE WITH A PLAUSIBLE EXCUSE IS STILL A RED GATE. The rematch gate went
    red and I built a good case for relaxing it: the rate is mostly arithmetic (16
    men, 3-card boards, deep runs), the old 20% only ever held because the board
    could pad from 24 fresh gatekeepers, a proxy that tracks run depth will punish
    you eventually. Every word true. Also beside the point — it was red because the
    TEST BOT ducked the title and ran the 40-fight guard every time. Fix the bot,
    it reads 12%, threshold untouched. I HAD THE ARGUMENT FOR MOVING THE GOALPOST
    BEFORE I HAD THE DIAGNOSIS, and that ordering is the tell. (The new card-level
    assertions are still worth having — they measure the word in the gate's own
    name — but they went in ALONGSIDE the 20%, not instead of it.)
  * THE HARNESS IS PART OF THE GAME, AND IT WAS LYING IN THREE PLACES. Every bot
    in every script ducked the title shot: sim-climb-runs' `easy` (offered on 28 of
    41 boards, taken 0 times), test-climb-fixes' middle-card bot, and the smoke
    test's bad bot. Each produced a confident number ABOUT THE GAME that was really
    a number about a bot refusing to win: 'safe' at 0%, a 46% rematch rate, 35-fight
    average runs. If a bot never finishes, everything it measures is about a state
    no player reaches.
  * WHEN A RUN WON'T END, CHECK THE WIN CONDITION FIRST. Four symptoms, an hour of
    tuning, and all of it downstream of "the champion is no longer in the pool."
    Run length was the tell from the first measurement and I read it as a balance
    problem for an hour.
  * SORT BEFORE YOU RELY ON AN ORDER. My own cap did `scored.filter(...)` then
    `.pop()` to "keep the hardest" — but scored is in BOARD order, so pop() kept
    whichever card was listed last. The comment said hardest, the code said last,
    nothing threw, and the favorite-hunter took the gift every time.
